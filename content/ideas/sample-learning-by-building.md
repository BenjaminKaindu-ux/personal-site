I keep coming back to the same observation: tutorials are comfortable, and building is not. The split is almost exactly the quality payoff.

Small theory: never learn a tool for its own sake — learn it to solve a problem you already have. The tool becomes the *means*, the problem becomes the memory hook, and the frustration stops feeling wasted.

Not a law, just a hunch. Filing it here for later as a proper essay.
